# Aura Clash RNG – pitch
Up: [[MOC-Aura-Clash]]

**Hook:** Roll for anime fighters, power them up with storm-born auras, and clash other players' teams.

**Audience:** ages ~10–15 (core), 16–20 (secondary). Phone-first (~70% of Roblox sessions), good on PC for creators. Target maturity: **Mild** (action, no gore).

**Why it can work:** proven anime-RNG loop players already search for + 2 easy-to-explain twists.

## Core loop (30 s)
Roll dice → get fighter (rarity, element) → place in a zone → earns cash (also offline) → buy better dice / slots → roll again

## Twist 1 – Auras (mutations)
- 3 zones at launch: 🌋 Volcano (Fire) · ⛈️ Storm Peak (Storm) · 🌑 Shadow Grove (Shadow)
- Weather events every ~10–15 min: Blood Moon, Thunder Storm, Void Rift
- Fighters in the matching zone may gain a glowing **aura** → stat + income multiplier
- Placement = a real choice. Aura reveals = clips.

## Twist 2 – Fair Clash (PvP)
- Pick 3 fighters → auto-battle another player's team (no aiming → fair on phones)
- Matchmaking by team power
- Elements: Fire > Shadow > Storm > Fire → smart cheap team can beat a rare one
- Win: **Luck Charge** (temporary roll luck, capped, fades) + Clash Tokens
- Lose: keep everything, small consolation
- Clash luck can't be bought with Robux

## Shared moment
Hourly **server boss**: everyone's fighters attack together, everyone gets dice drops.

## Clip moments
Secret roll (slow-mo dice, flash, server announce) · aura storm hitting your zone · underdog Clash win

## First 60 s
5 s roll free die → 10 s guaranteed Rare reveal → 20 s arrow to zone, place, cash ticks → 60 s buy 2nd die → 3 min first Clash vs bot (win)

## Monetization (fair) → skill `roblox-ethical-monetization-policy`
Starter pack (after first upgrade) · Auto-roll pass · VIP · extra slots · luck potions for normal rolls (odds shown) · cosmetic auras/dice skins
