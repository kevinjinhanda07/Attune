# Steal a Brainrot
Up: [[MOC-Case-Studies]] · Patterns: [[Hit-Game-Patterns]]

> Mechanics and lessons only. Use them as inspiration for **original** designs.

- **Status:** 2025 mega-hit; reported ~25M peak CCU (Sep 2025); Innovation Award (Creative Direction)
- **Genre:** Collect / steal tycoon (meme)
- **Core loop:** Buy characters off a conveyor → they generate cash in your base → lock base → run into other bases and carry their characters home
- **Session:** Short raids inside long idle sessions
- **Social:** Theft + defense creates rivalry; trading; huge creator coverage
- **Monetization:** Speed/lock time boosts, character purchases, admin-style event hype
- **Update cadence:** Weekly updates + live events, a cross-game 'admin war' with [[Grow-a-Garden]]
- **Why it spread:** Meme characters (Italian 'brainrot' trend) made every clip shareable; simple loop readable in 3 s

## Lessons
- Ride a culture trend with original characters
- a visible rarity ladder on a conveyor = instant FOMO
- stealing must be physical and counterable
