# Adopt Me!
Up: [[MOC-Case-Studies]] · Patterns: [[Hit-Game-Patterns]]

> Mechanics and lessons only. Use them as inspiration for **original** designs.

- **Status:** Evergreen pet/roleplay; long-time top game
- **Genre:** Pet collecting + roleplay
- **Core loop:** Care for pets (tasks) → earn money → hatch eggs → age pets up → trade for rarer pets
- **Session:** Medium, social
- **Social:** Trading is the core social game, homes, roleplay
- **Monetization:** Eggs, pets, potions, houses
- **Update cadence:** Regular themed events and limited pets
- **Why it spread:** Pets + trading + social hangout; limited-time pets fuel demand

## Lessons
- Limited-time items keep an economy alive
- trading needs strong anti-scam UI
