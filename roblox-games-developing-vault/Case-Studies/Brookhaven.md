# Brookhaven RP
Up: [[MOC-Case-Studies]] · Patterns: [[Hit-Game-Patterns]]

> Mechanics and lessons only. Use them as inspiration for **original** designs.

- **Status:** Evergreen #1-tier roleplay
- **Genre:** Social roleplay sandbox
- **Core loop:** Pick house/job/vehicle → roleplay with others → customize
- **Session:** Open-ended
- **Social:** Pure social: families, stories, hangouts
- **Monetization:** Premium houses/vehicles passes
- **Update cadence:** Steady content drops
- **Why it spread:** Zero-friction roleplay for young players; mobile-friendly

## Lessons
- Sometimes the game is just a stage – give players props and space
