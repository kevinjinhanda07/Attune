# Plants vs Brainrots
Up: [[MOC-Case-Studies]] · Patterns: [[Hit-Game-Patterns]]

> Mechanics and lessons only. Use them as inspiration for **original** designs.

- **Status:** 2025 breakout (late Sep 2025)
- **Genre:** Lane defense + collection
- **Core loop:** Plant seeds → plants defend against walking characters → defeated ones join your base and earn cash → buy rarer seeds
- **Session:** Short waves, idle earning
- **Social:** Trading rare plants/characters, meme hype
- **Monetization:** Seeds, boosts
- **Update cadence:** Fast updates riding the meme wave
- **Why it spread:** Combined two trends (garden + brainrot) into a mash-up

## Lessons
- Genre mash-ups of two hot trends can break out fast
- must move fast before the trend fades
