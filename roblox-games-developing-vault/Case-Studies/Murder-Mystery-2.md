# Murder Mystery 2
Up: [[MOC-Case-Studies]] · Patterns: [[Hit-Game-Patterns]]

> Mechanics and lessons only. Use them as inspiration for **original** designs.

- **Status:** Evergreen social deduction; ~700K peak CCU claimed
- **Genre:** Round-based social deduction
- **Core loop:** Random roles (murderer, sheriff, innocents) → round → coins → crates/skins → trade
- **Session:** ~3 min rounds
- **Social:** Friends, bluffing, trading knives/guns skins
- **Monetization:** Crates, cosmetic weapons
- **Update cadence:** Holiday events with limited skins
- **Why it spread:** Simple roles, instant drama, huge trading scene

## Lessons
- Hidden roles = endless replay
- cosmetics + seasonal limited items = long tail
