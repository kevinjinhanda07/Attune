# 99 Nights in the Forest
Up: [[MOC-Case-Studies]] · Patterns: [[Hit-Game-Patterns]]

> Mechanics and lessons only. Use them as inspiration for **original** designs.

- **Status:** 2025 horror-survival hit; ~14M all-time peak; Best Horror award
- **Genre:** Co-op survival horror
- **Core loop:** Gather wood/food by day → keep the campfire alive at night → rescue lost kids → upgrade fire/crafting → survive to night 99
- **Session:** 30–60 min runs, can rejoin
- **Social:** Squad of friends, roles/classes, shared panic moments
- **Monetization:** Classes, cosmetics, boosts (check current)
- **Update cadence:** Regular updates adding biomes/enemies/events
- **Why it spread:** Clear goal in the title (survive 99 nights); co-op scares = great reaction clips

## Lessons
- A number in the title is a promise and a progress bar
- co-op horror makes clips
- day/night rhythm = tension curve
