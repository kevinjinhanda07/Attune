# Level design basics
Up: [[MOC-World]] · Design: [[MOC-Design]]

1. Blockout in grey parts, scale to 5-stud character.
2. Landmark visible from spawn → players orient.
3. Light/color leads along the critical path.
4. Rhythm: challenge → rest → reward.
5. Loops back to hub; shortcuts unlock later.
6. Kill/teleport volumes under the map.
7. Playtest with friends before art.
