Terms

Last updated November 4, 2026

Using Attune

Attune is a free tool that gives feedback on writing. You can use it without an account. Creating an account lets you save your scans.

Your writing

What you write stays yours. When you save a scan, we store it only so you can open it again. We don't sell it, share it or use it to train AI models.

Feedback is a suggestion

Attune's scores and tips come from automatic checks and are not always right. Use your own judgment, and follow your teacher's, school's or workplace's rules about writing tools.

Fair use

Don't use Attune to break the law, to attack or overload the service, or to get into other people's accounts. We may close accounts that do.

Changes and availability

Attune is provided as it is, without guarantees. Features may change, and we will update this page if these terms change. Paid plans don't exist yet; if they are added, they will be optional and described here first.

Your account

You can delete saved scans at any time from My scans. To close your account, contact us through github.com/kevinjinhanda07/Attune. See the Privacy policy for what we store.
