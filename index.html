How it works

Three steps, while you write

Attune follows the order writing teachers recommend: define the reader first, then judge the writing against them.

1

Describe your reader

Who they are, what they already know, what they need from you. Pick a preset or write your own.

2

Scan your writing

Attune reads each paragraph and colors it: green fits, yellow drifts, pink loses your reader.

3

Fix what needs work

Go to each paragraph in order. Swap jargon in one click, edit, and re-check.

What it checks

Attune for Chrome

Use it in every Google Doc

Install once. A small Attune button appears in the corner of every document you open. Click it for scores, color-coded paragraphs and Go to next.

Download from GitHub
  1. Download attune-chrome-extension.zip from GitHub and unzip it somewhere you'll keep it.
  2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  4. Open any Google Doc and click the Attune button in the bottom-right corner.
FAQ

Questions

Is Attune an AI detector?

No. Attune doesn't guess who wrote something. It checks whether the writing works for the person who'll read it.

Is my writing stored or sent anywhere?

The scan on this page runs entirely in your browser. Deep review, in the extension, sends only the paragraph you choose, with its neighbors and your reader description, to Anthropic's API using your own key.

Are the scores grades?

No. They're estimates to help you revise. A score of 83 and a score of 85 aren't meaningfully different. Read the labels and the fixes, and trust your own judgment.

Does it penalize technical words or informal English?

Not on their own. Technical terms are fine when your reader knows them, and Attune doesn't treat one style of English as the correct one. It flags words only when your reader is likely to get lost.

What does it cost?

The scan is free. Deep review uses your own Anthropic API key and costs a few cents per paragraph.
