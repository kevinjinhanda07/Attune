# Emil Kowalski Design Skills

Animation and design-engineering skills from Emil Kowalski (maker of Sonner and Vaul; has worked at Vercel and Linear).

## What it does
- `animate`, `improve-animations`, `review-animations`, `choreograph`, `find-animation-opportunities`, `remove-animations`, `animation-vocabulary`, `apple-design`, `break-ui`, `prototype`.
- Teaches when *not* to animate, easing/duration choices, interruptible motion: the Linear/Vercel feel.
