Before you shop

Can I use a promo code on sale items?

No. Sale prices already include the discount, so codes can't be added on top. Points and the free mini set still apply.

Is shipping free?

Standard shipping is free on orders over $35 all weekend. Orders placed by Monday at midnight leave the warehouse within two days.

What if I want to return something?

Anything bought between 25 and 30 November can be returned until 15 January, in store or by post, opened or not.

Is there a limit on how many I can buy?

Doorbusters are limited to two of each product per customer. Everything else has no limit while stock lasts.

I bought something last week. Do I get the sale price?

Yes. Bring your receipt or ask online and we'll refund the difference on anything bought from 13 November on.
